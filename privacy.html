Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Cleaning Echo (“we”, “us”) collects and uses information when you visit cleaningecho.com or contact us.

Information we collect

When you request a quote or contact us by phone, WhatsApp or our website form, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, city or property address, and details about the cleaning you need.

How we use it

We use this information only to respond to you, prepare quotes, schedule and provide our services, and communicate with you about your bookings.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it with service providers that help us operate (for example, messaging or form tools), or when required by law.

Your choices
