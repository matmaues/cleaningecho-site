Frequently asked questions

What’s included in a cleaning?

Our standard cleaning typically includes dusting surfaces, cleaning bathrooms, wiping kitchen counters and exterior appliances, making beds, emptying trash, and vacuuming and/or mopping floors in common areas and bedrooms. For deep cleanings, move-in/move-out, or Airbnb turnovers, we add extra attention to details like baseboards, inside cabinets (when requested), buildup in kitchens and bathrooms, and high-touch surfaces.

Can I skip or reschedule bookings?

Yes. Just let us know as early as possible (ideally at least 24 hours in advance). If you’re on a recurring plan, we’ll help you find the next best date and time.

How long should a house cleaning take?

A standard cleaning for a typical 1–2 bedroom home usually takes around 2–3 hours; larger homes or deep cleanings can take longer. For Airbnb turnovers, we work within the check-out/check-in window.

Do you bring supplies?

Yes, we bring our own professional products and equipment.

What languages do you speak?

We speak English, Portuguese & Spanish.

Are you pet-friendly?

Yes! We’re happy to clean homes with pets.

Do you use eco-friendly products?

Yes, eco-friendly products are available on request.
