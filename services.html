Our Services

Cleaning for homes, short-term rentals and offices

🧽

Regular Cleaning (Maintenance)

We provide high‑standard maintenance cleaning designed to keep spaces consistently clean, organized, and welcoming. This service includes surface cleaning, floors, kitchens, bathrooms, and common areas, ensuring continuity, comfort, and ongoing excellence for residential and commercial properties.

✨

Deep Cleaning

Our deep cleaning service is ideal for spaces that require detailed attention. We focus on removing built‑up dirt, grease, heavy dust, and stubborn residues, with special care given to kitchens, bathrooms, floors, baseboards, and high‑touch surfaces, delivering a fully refreshed environment.

📦

Move In / Move Out Cleaning

We offer comprehensive cleaning services for move‑in and move‑out situations, ensuring the property is immaculate, sanitized, and ready for new occupants or final inspection. The service covers floors, kitchens, bathrooms, cabinets, appliances, and all surfaces, meeting high real estate and property management standards.

🏠

Airbnb Cleaning Service

We provide specialized cleaning services for short-term rental properties, focused on excellence, consistency, and meticulous attention to detail. Each space is carefully cleaned, sanitized, and prepared to welcome guests with comfort and high standards. Our service includes detailed cleaning of bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchens, and common areas, ensuring an outstanding guest experience and positive reviews for hosts and properties.

🏢

Office & Commercial Cleaning

A clean workplace makes a great first impression. We clean offices and commercial spaces, including desks and surfaces, floors, restrooms, break rooms and common areas, on a schedule that fits your business hours.

🏗️

Post-Construction Cleaning

After a renovation or new build, we remove fine dust, debris and marks so the space is polished and ready to move into. We carefully clean floors, baseboards, surfaces, cabinets and finishes.
